The short version

How Catalyst runs

Four areas, short version below. Staff will point you back here before anywhere else.

Conduct
  • Be respectful — no hate speech, extreme toxicity, or targeted harassment.
  • Staff decisions are final. Take disputes to DMs or a ticket, not public chat.
Combat
  • PvP is enabled — fight freely, but no spawn-camping or relentless targeting.
  • No flooding bases or terrain with water, lava, or ice — cobble monsters and obsidian griefs are banned too. This is a hard rule, not a judgment call.
  • No cheating. X-ray, hacked clients, and dupes are an instant ban. Sodium, Iris, and minimaps are fine — those aren't cheats.
Builds & land
  • Base spacing: build at least 150+ blocks from your nearest neighbor, unless you've both agreed otherwise.
  • Keep it clean — repair creeper holes, chop entire trees, remove pillar towers.
Creator quota
  • Catalyst is a creator server — post at least once a month featuring server content. Long-form (YouTube), a livestream (Twitch/YouTube/Kick), or a short (Shorts/TikTok/Reels) all count.
